## 📋 Overview

DIH-MIS (Darusihha International Hospital Management Information System) v12 is a comprehensive, cloud-ready hospital management platform designed specifically for healthcare facilities in Somalia and East Africa.

### ✨ Key Features

✅ **Patient Management** - Register, track, and manage patient records  
✅ **OPD Clinic** - Outpatient department management with consultation tracking  
✅ **Emergency Department** - Critical care triage and emergency patient handling  
✅ **Laboratory** - Diagnostic test ordering and result reporting  
✅ **Pharmacy** - Medication inventory and prescription management  
✅ **Billing System** - Invoice generation and payment tracking  
✅ **OB/GYN Services** - Maternal and gynecological care modules  
✅ **AI Assistant** - Dr. Thani AI for medical guidance (Somali language support)  
✅ **Data Backup** - Export and restore system data as JSON  
✅ **MOH Compliant** - Meets Ministry of Health compliance standards 🇸🇴

## 📤 Backup Your Data

### **Export Data (Create Backup)**
1. Click **System Backup** tab
2. Click **DOWNLOAD MASTER BACKUP**
3. A `.json` file downloads
4. Keep it safe!

### **Restore Data (From Backup)**
1. Click **System Backup** tab
2. Click **UPLOAD & RESTORE BACKUP**
3. Select your `.json` backup file
4. Data restored instantly!

## 🎯 Quick Start Example

### **Register a Patient:**
1. Click **Reception** menu
2. Fill: Name, Age, Gender, Phone, Department, Complaint
3. Click **ADMIT PATIENT TO CLINIC**
4. ✅ Done! Patient appears in dashboard

### **Create Invoice:**
1. Click **Billing** menu
2. Select patient from unpaid list
3. Add lab fees, pharmacy fees
4. Review total amount
5. Click **MARK AS PAID**
6. Print if needed

### **Add Medicine:**
1. Click **Pharmacy** menu
2. Click **+ New Item**
3. Enter: Medicine name, Unit, Quantity, Price
4. Click **Add to Inventory**
5. ✅ Medicine added!
